## 6. Judgements the packet asked for explicitly

- **W4's deliberate divergence — one automatic re-run from `analysis.unknown_attempt`.** **Sound; no finding.** Plan §10's own `AI_ATTEMPT_UNCERTAIN` row says "tái chạy **theo policy** ghi attempt mới", and §8.2 says only that no two *committed results* share a key while explicitly declining to promise once-only external inference — so an automatic re-run is delegated to PC03, not forbidden. It is bounded: `analysis_unknown_attempt_auto_rerun = 1` **consumes** one unit of `analysis_attempts_per_item = 2`, and constraint RPC-08 (`auto_rerun <= attempts_per_item − 1`) pins that, so D43's "thử lại một lần" survives — total model calls per `(analysis_key, generation)` remain 2. The asymmetry against `delivery.unknown` (never auto-retried) is argued from consequence rather than convenience: an uncertain analysis costs money, an uncertain delivery puts a duplicate in front of a person and cannot be undone or observed. The re-run writes a new attempt row and leaves the old one `cost_uncertain = true` (I14). Labelled PROVISIONAL. I would accept this.
- **Checkpoint one-account test (R-01).** **Consistent.** PC01 `ingest.commit_checkpoint` (restricted, `item_count: 0`, guard, `VALIDATION_ERROR`), PC02 `TXN-checkpoint-only` (append-only row, receipt `checkpoint_kind = 'checkpoint_only'`, `acked_through` unchanged), PC03 CP-01/CP-07/FT-CHECKPOINT-ONLY and I02's reworded counterexample 2 all state the same rule and the same oracle — `checkpoint.acked_through_ingest_sequence <= MAX(committed ingest_receipt.max_ingest_sequence)`. The single residual mismatch is the CP-07 sentence in `F-A1R2-04`.
- **Backfill keyed by normalised tag text (W5) vs D28/B04.** **Correct reading; no finding on the choice itself.** SRC-SPEC §8.2 row 4 says the backfill is "đúng một lần" and offers "quét lại kho" as the escape hatch for deeper digging. Keying by `tag.id` would grant a fresh entitlement on every remove-and-re-add, which empties "exactly once" of meaning — PC04 states this rejection explicitly and correctly diagnoses why (the partial unique index mints a new `tag.id` per re-add). The acknowledged consequence — renaming a tag's text yields a new entitlement — is recorded as intentional rather than hidden, and is the honest position for a system that must not guess that two strings are the same interest. The defect is not the rule but that the schema cannot yet enforce it (`F-A1R2-03`).
- **Empty period: choice (b) with `abort_reason` (CR-PC03-06), and its replay argument.** **The argument is sound on its own terms and I verified both premises, but its anchor is missing.** I confirmed independently that `coverage_window` has no idempotency column (fields: `id, owner_id, sequence, window_from, window_to, predecessor_window_id, report_id, advanced_at`), so under option (a) a replayed `report.publish` after a lost ACK would indeed find no receipt, attempt a second coverage advance, lose CAS and return `CONFLICT` — exactly the failure SRC-PLAN §5.1 warns about. Choosing (b) to keep a durable anchor is the better engineering call, and PC04 discloses that it is departing from the Coordinator's recommendation, states the condition under which (a) becomes viable, and shows the Coordinator's actual concern (ambiguous `aborted`) is met by the five-value `abort_reason` enum. **However** the anchor it relies on, `report_build_id` on the `report` row, does not exist in the entity contract (`F-A1R2-01`), and `abort_reason` does not either (`F-A1R2-03`). So the reasoning is right and the artefacts are not yet consistent with it.
- **`report.schema.json` consistency with the empty-period choice.** **Consistent.** `status` is `const "published"` with `additionalProperties: false` and 17 required properties, so no read model can exist for a `building` or `aborted` row — which is exactly what makes both options (a) and (b) satisfy D57/AMD-B04 identically from the user's side, as §4.7.1 claims.
- **Threshold `PROVISIONAL_BOOTSTRAP` vs REQ-OQ08 and "no TBD".** **Exemplary; no finding.** REQ-OQ08 forbids stating a number as if measured; baseline §3 forbids leaving it blank. PC04 satisfies both: `0.8000` labelled `PROVISIONAL_BOOTSTRAP` with an operational (not evidential) rationale, plus `threshold_calibration_state = 'uncalibrated'` set by the A2 process rather than by the user, a required `threshold_calibration_evidence_ref`, propagation of the uncalibrated state into every report's `coverage_note` and the UI, an explicit prohibition on claiming any SRC-SPEC §1.4 quality metric while uncalibrated, and a locked-rubric/separate-set gate. The same treatment is given to `research_connector_rate_limit`, whose four unknown values are `PLACEHOLDER_KC` under REQ-A6 with a conservative `min_interval_ms = 3000` floor and `Retry-After` always winning — an honest "we do not know this yet" rather than a fabricated number.
